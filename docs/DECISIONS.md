# Highway 90 redesign: decisions and open items

A redesign **pitch** of thehighway90.com (Highway 90 Dispensary, Marlton, NJ). Every page of the live site's content
sitemaps is rebuilt at its own URL, with its own copy. The redesign changes the presentation, not the content.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Scope.** Every URL in the content sitemaps is rebuilt: pages, posts, events, stores, the 45 recreational town pages, specials, categories, strains, effects, terpenes, cannabinoids and every brand page. Of the menu's 3,166 product pages, 90+ are rebuilt as a snapshot. | The 3,166 product pages are generated by the menu feed. `audit/sitemap-inventory.json` records all ~3,760 live URLs by type: that is the architecture record. |
| D2 | **Copy is verbatim.** Headings, body copy, FAQs, reviews, deals, hours and contact facts come from the live pages. Section labels on the road signs ("Categories", "Google reviews", "Good to know") are navigation labels, not claims. | Preserve before improving. |
| D3 | **Brand kept.** The live palette (green #50804C, deep green #05371E, orange #E47124, cream #F3F1E5), the live fonts (Rubik, Heebo, Damion), the HWY 90 route-shield badge, the road-sign logo and the orange New Jersey road map. | The brief: a total redesign that keeps the brand and its tone. |
| D4 | **Style: "Route 90 Pop".** Frosted glass panels with ink outlines and hard offset shadows (sticker style), Ben-Day halftone fields, starburst deal badges, highway-sign section heads, lane-line dividers and a lane-line scroll bar. | The brief asked for glassmorphism plus pop art, clean and modern. |
| D5 | **Pop art without characters.** Pop art is applied as graphic devices: halftone, colour blocks, outlines and bursts. There are no cartoons, mascots or comic figures, and no people appear in generated media. | Cannabis advertising rules restrict imagery that appeals to people under 21. This reading of the New Jersey rules is not a legal review. |
| D6 | **Depth.** Up to five layers: loop or map backdrop, halftone, glass, then cut-outs (buds, a leaf, a pre-roll, the route shield) that cross section seams with scroll parallax. | The brief asked for layers and images protruding across sections. |
| D7 | **Motion.** Scroll reveals (rise, wipe, word-by-word headlines), parallax cut-outs, a deals ticker, and pointer-tilt plus press/lift hovers on every card and button. `prefers-reduced-motion` stops all of it, and every loop has a pause button. | The brief asked for scroll animations and hover effects. |
| D8 | **Imagery.** The real photos and deal graphics are kept: the storefront, the effect photos, the gift card, the specials banners and the product packshots. Added fal images cover category art and moods, and 3 Higgsfield loops add motion (a highway at sunset, night light trails, smoke). All AI media carries an IPTC "trainedAlgorithmicMedia" label. | Real photos are never replaced by generated ones, and generated media never shows the store, staff or a real product. |
| D9 | **Menu snapshot.** The live product rails and menus are rendered client-side by the Joint plugin, which cannot run off the client's domain. The rails show real products read from the live product pages (name, brand, sizes, prices, photos) as a static snapshot. Which products each rail shows is the preview's choice, not the live menu's. | The rails need real products to be presentable. Going live means wiring the Joint/Dutchie menu back in. |
| D10 | **SEO carried.** Each page keeps its live title, description, canonical, Open Graph tags and the live JSON-LD (verbatim). The two live 301s are carried as redirect stubs plus `_redirects`. | Preserve the SEO surface. |
| D11 | **No platform runtime.** No WordPress, Elementor, LiteSpeed, analytics or Joint scripts. The build is plain HTML/CSS/JS from a Node-only generator. | Reconstruct the result, not the platform. |
| D12 | **Phone pass** (`src/styles/mobile.css`, loaded last), measured at 390×844 and 360×780 on every template:<br>• Tighter section rhythm and a compact header.<br>• Rails are swipe-only, with a peek of the next card; the arrow buttons are hidden.<br>• Cut-outs are pinned to the gutters, with no scroll parallax on phones.<br>• The menu snapshot and the sub-category cards are two-up.<br>• Long SEO copy folds behind "Read more". Every word stays in the page, and keyboard focus opens the fold. Articles never fold.<br>• Opening hours read as rows.<br>• The footer has two link columns and 40px+ tap targets. | Revision 1: the operator asked to "cleanup mobile". Phone page lengths fell by 10–58%; for example, the category page went from 9,651px to 4,039px. |
| D13 | **Product descriptions repaired.** The live product JSON-LD was saved with its backslashes stripped ("u2019", "&lt;br /&gt;"), so 36 of 101 descriptions read as code. The snapshot now decodes those characters, emoji included, and the line breaks. | The text stays the live text: only its encoding is repaired. |

## Open items for Highway 90

| # | Item |
|---|---|
| B1 | **Social links.** The live footer shows Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn icons that link nowhere. They are left out until the real profile URLs are supplied. |
| B2 | **Inconsistent first-visit offer.** The home page says 35% / 20% / 25%; the recreational town pages say 15% / 20% / 25%. Both are carried verbatim. Which is current? |
| B3 | **Duplicated product titles.** Some live product H1s repeat the brand ("Fernway Fernway Honeycrisp PRO Disposable"). Carried verbatim. |
| B4 | **Dead links inherited from the live site.** These fail on the live site too: 5 expired specials (for example `/specials/pre-roll-bundle-3-for-30/`), `/menu/featured/` (404), 2 discontinued products linked from a blog post, a typo link `/categoires/flower/`, a relative link `/categories/flower//categories/pre-rolls/` and two `#advertising` anchors. `/blog/whatisgreenwednesday/` is live but missing from the sitemap; it is rebuilt. |
| B5 | **Forms and accounts.** Contact, rewards sign-up/sign-in, My Points, My Orders and search need their backends (Joint/Springbig). The preview forms post nowhere and start disabled. |
| B6 | **Menu integration.** See D9: the live Joint menu (store 6696) must be connected on the production domain. |
| B7 | **Typekit.** The live site loads Heebo from Adobe Typekit. The redesign self-hosts the open-source Heebo and Rubik instead. |
| B8 | **Agency credit.** The previous agency's footer logo is not carried. |
| B9 | **Compliance review.** Deal copy, effect claims and the pop-art direction should be reviewed against the CRC advertising rules before launch. |

## What was checked (fast track)

- **Crawl.** 699 live pages returned 200, plus 2 carried 301s. The 5×404 breaker stopped the first pass on the site's own dead links; there was no lockout and no refusal.
- **Extraction recall** (model against live page): median 1.0, with 688 of 697 pages at 0.97 or above. The 9 below are the Events Calendar pages, where date chips and "event has passed" notices are not modelled. The positive control fires.
- **Link check** over 2,006 files, with a positive control:
  - every internal page link resolves, except the inherited dead links in B4;
  - each page's `og:image` is the live wp-content URL, carried verbatim.
- **JS errors:** 0 across 14 pages of every type.
- **Phone overflow probe:** innerWidth and scrollWidth are 390 at 390.
- **Nested-link scan:** 0 nested anchors.
- **Screenshots:** 1440×900, 1280×585 and 390×844.

The full site-reforge gate (`sr-gate`) was not run. This is a pitch.
