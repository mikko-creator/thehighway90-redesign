What Is Green Wednesday? Highway 90’s Biggest Cannabis Savings Are Almost Here!

November 22, 2024

What is Green Wednesday? 🍃

Known as the “Black Friday” of cannabis, Green Wednesday has become a major day for weed enthusiasts and savvy shoppers to stock up on their favorite products before the holiday season and makes an excellent opportunity to grab your favorite brands and capitalize on once-a-year deals. The cannabis holiday began in California around 2016 when dispensaries noticed an increase in sales on the day before Thanksgiving. As many Americans prepare to embark on long weekends filled with family, food, and travel, one thing is clear: cannabis has a way of making these moments even better and is becoming an essential part of holiday traditions for many.

Here’s what you can expect at Highway 90:

Exclusive Deals and Discounts 💰

Get ready for unbeatable savings on premium cannabis products all week long that you won’t find anywhere else. From flower and concentrates to edibles and accessories, we’ve got something for everyone. Whether you’re hosting a Friendsgiving, looking to unwind after a long day of cooking, or just stocking up, Highway 90’s deals will help you save big while enjoying top-quality products.

Ask your budtender about the sales Highway 90 is offering and treat yourself for the holiday. Whether you need gummies for grandma or pre-rolls for your pre-Thanksgiving dinner walk, our unbeatable deals have something for everyone to enjoy! The countdown has begun, and with exclusive deals on the horizon, you find your favorite products at a price that’s too good to pass up. Stay tuned to learn more about Highway 90’s weeklong deals!

Expert Recommendations 🧑‍🏫

Not sure what to pick up? Our knowledgeable budtenders will be on hand to guide you through the best options for your needs. Looking for something to spark creativity, enhance relaxation, or make the holiday more festive? We’ve got the perfect strains and products to match. Our team is ready to provide expert product recommendations tailored just to you, ensuring you find the perfect items for your needs. Don’t forget to check out our flower bud-bar to find new, high quality strains picked exclusively by our expert budtenders.

Perfect Timing for Thanksgiving Prep 🦃

With Thanksgiving the next day, it’s the perfect time to pick up everything you need to enhance your holiday. Whether you’re looking for an uplifting strain to help with cooking, or an edible to relax after dinner, Highway 90’s got you covered. Hosting friends and family? Highway 90 will ensure you’re fully prepared with cannabis products that complement your Thanksgiving weekend – whether you’re chilling or celebrating.

Why Shop on Green Wednesday? 🍃

It’s quickly become one of the most popular shopping days for cannabis enthusiasts, just in time for Thanksgiving. With exclusive discounts and an opportunity to try new products, it’s the perfect time to treat yourself – or grab gifts for the cannabis lovers in your life.
