Highway 90’s 2nd Annual 4/20 Fest

April 11, 2025

Highway 90’s 2nd Annual 4/20 Fest: Three Days. One Epic Celebration. All at Highway 90 in Marlton!

420 is more than just a date—it’s a culture, a vibe, and a time to come together and celebrate the plant we all love. This year, we’re going BIGGER and BETTER with our 2nd Annual 4/20 Weekend Celebration, and you’re invited!

While we’re keeping our biggest deals under wraps for now 👀, we can promise an unforgettable weekend packed with store-wide savings, awesome giveaways, live entertainment, and a stacked schedule of brand pop-ups and local flavor.

💥 Here’s What to Expect:

🍃 Premium Brand & Vendor Pop-Ups
Meet your favorite cannabis brands face-to-face, learn more about their products, and score exclusive swag with your favorite products.

🎶 Live Music All Weekend
Feel the vibe with local musicians bringing the energy—from chill afternoon sets to upbeat evening jams.

🌭 Local Eats & Food Trucks
Come hungry! We’re featuring some of the best local food trucks serving up hot dogs, cheesesteaks, and other munchie must-haves.

🛍️ Goodie Bags & Giveaways
Early birds and lucky winners will get hooked up with limited edition swag, branded goodies, and maybe even a few surprises.

🔥 Massive Store-Wide Discounts
We can’t reveal the full list just yet, but trust us—you’ll want to stock up.

✨ Exclusive Products & Surprise Drops
Be on the lookout for one-time drops and limited items only available during our 4/20 celebration.

📅 4/20 Weekend Lineup:

Friday, April 18
Kick things off with early deals, music, and your favorite brands.

  • Stashhouse/Victory Farms: Drool, Major & Cheeba Chews | 12:30–2:30 PM

  • High Grass Farms / Jersey Landscapes | 4–6 PM

  • Edie Parker | 6–8 PM

  • 🎉 Store-wide discounts begin!

Saturday, April 19
Vibes in full swing—live music, munchies, and more brands in-store.

  • Little Leaf Labs /The Growfather  | 3–5 PM

  • 🧀 The Cheesesteak Guy food truck

  • 🎵 Live music throughout the day

  • 🛍️ Ongoing store-wide deals

Sunday, April 20 (The Big Day!)
The main event! Celebrate 4/20 the right way—with good vibes, good music, good food, and amazing deals.

  • Clade9 | 12–2 PM

  • Jersey Smooth | 12–2 PM

  • 🌭 Rocks Dogs serving up hot dogs

  • 🎸 Live Music

  • 🔥 Store-wide discounts all day long!

✅ Save the Date. Spread the Word. Bring Your Friends.

Whether you’re stocking up, vibing out, or just stopping by to enjoy the scene—Highway 90 is the place to be this 4/20 weekend. Follow us on social and subscribe to our emails so you’re first to know when our official deals drop!

Let’s make this 4/20 one to remember. See you there! 💚
