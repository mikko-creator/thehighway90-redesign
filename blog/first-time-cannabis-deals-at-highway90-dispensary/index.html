These discounts apply every day, so be sure to bring your ID and any relevant documentation to save even more!

Please remember that all deals and discounts will be applied at the register. If you don’t see a discount applied, don’t hesitate to ask your budtender for help. Note that discounts can’t be stacked, but we’ll always ensure you get the best deal available!

How to Shop at Highway 90 Dispensary

Shopping at Highway 90 is straightforward and stress-free. Whether you’re a first-time visitor or a regular customer, we’ve designed the process to be easy, efficient, and enjoyable.

Pre-Order or Browse In-Store

Not sure where to start? No worries! You can choose to either pre-order online for a quick pickup or come in-store to browse our selection with the help of one of our friendly, knowledgeable budtenders. They’re here to help answer any questions, guide you through our products, and offer recommendations based on your preferences.

We accept cash, debit & credit payments through the Highway 90 app, Dutchie Pay, and have on-site ATMs for your convenience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who Can Shop at Highway 90?
Cannabis is legal in New Jersey for adults aged 21 and older. Highway 90 is a recreational dispensary, but we also offer a 10% discount for medical patients from any state with proof of medical ID.

What Can You Buy at Highway 90?
We offer a wide range of premium cannabis products, including:

    • Flower
    • Edibles
    • Concentrates
    • Vapes
    • Pre-Rolls
    • And more!

All of our products undergo rigorous testing to ensure they meet the highest quality and safety standards. New Jersey has specific purchasing limits, which include:

  • 1 ounce of flower
  • 4 grams of concentrates, oils, or vapes
  • 1,000 mg of edibles

You can also mix and match these products, as long as your total doesn’t exceed the NJ state limit.

How Can I Pay for My Order?
Due to current banking restrictions in New Jersey, debit and credit cards may not always be accepted. However, we gladly accept cash, Dutchie Pay, payments via the Highway 90 app using credit or debit cards, or ATM withdrawals on-site.

What are Highway 90’s Store Hours?

Our store hours are Monday-Saturday 9am-10pm and Sunday 9am-7pm. We’re open every day, and we’re here to help you find the perfect products for your needs. Hours are subject to change on Holidays or due to inclement weather.

What’s the difference between recreational and medical cannabis?

As a recreational dispensary, our cannabis products are available to all adults 21 and over with valid ID, while medical cannabis is specifically for patients with qualifying conditions who hold a valid medical marijuana card. At Highway 90, we offer 10% off for medical patients from any state with valid and up to date proof of their medical marijuana card.

How do I know what products are best for me?

If you’re unsure where to start, our experienced budtenders are here to help. They can guide you based on your preferences and what you’re looking for – whether that’s energy, relaxation, pain relief, sleep, or something else! Just ask, and our staff will be more than happy to assist you.

Does Highway 90 carry CBD products?

Yes! We carry a variety of CBD products, including tinctures, edibles, and topicals. Our budtenders can help you choose the best product for your needs.

How do I redeem my rewards points?

You can redeem your rewards points for discounts on your purchases once you reach 100 points. For every dollar spent, you earn 1 point, and once you reach 100, you’ll get $5 off your next order. You can check your points balance through our Highway 90 app or ask our budtenders for an update. Our budtenders can also tell you at the register how many points you have, and will always ask if you want to apply your available rewards to your purchase.

Can I return or exchange products?

All sales are final. No exchanges or returns unless defective. Defective products will only be exchanged within 7 days of purchase. You must provide the original packaging and product, as well as your receipt.

How Can I Make the Most of My Visit to Highway 90?

Come with an open mind and any questions you may have! Whether you’re here for your first time or you’re a regular, our friendly budtenders are ready to guide you to the perfect products. At Highway 90 Dispensary, we’re all about good vibes and helping you discover exactly what you need to elevate your experience. So, let’s make it a great visit – we’ve got the best products and deals waiting for you!

Want to stay updated on our latest specials and product drops? Join our rewards program today and get notified about the hottest deals and newest arrivals at Highway 90. You won’t want to miss out on our exclusive offers!
