Cannabis Flower FAQs

What exactly is Cannabis Flower?

Flower serves as an alternate term for the mature blooms of the cannabis plant. These buds, harvested for smoking, encapsulate the psychoactive and therapeutic properties of THC, CBD, and various cannabinoids. Unlock the full potential of cannabis with these potent and versatile flower buds.

Why is Cannabis called a Flower?

In the realm of cannabis, the term “flower” has gained prominence as a more specific product identifier. It is interchangeable with other terms such as bud, pot, and weed within the cannabis lexicon. This shift in language reflects a growing precision in describing and categorizing different parts of the cannabis plant.

How long do the effects of Cannabis Flower last?

Typically, the peak effects of smoking flower, commonly associated with the “high,” manifest within a timeframe ranging from 10 minutes to two hours post-smoking. As the effects gradually subside around the one to two-hour mark. Optimize your cannabis experience by understanding the duration of effects and tailoring your consumption accordingly.

How is the potency of Cannabis Flower measured?

Cannabis flower potency is commonly assessed through its THC (tetrahydrocannabinol) content, the primary psychoactive compound accountable for the associated “high.” The measurement of cannabis flower potency is expressed as a percentage of THC by weight.

Does Cannabis Flower expire?

Similar to any organic substance, cannabis flower has a shelf life. With time, the potency and flavor of aging cannabis flower may diminish, leading to dryness and brittleness. To preserve freshness, store cannabis flower in an airtight container in a cool, dark location. Proper storage can extend its lifespan for several months.

How do you store Cannabis Flower?

When exposed to light and air, cannabis flower may undergo changes in potency and flavor, leading to a less desirable experience. An airtight container serves as a protective shield against these external elements, creating an environment conducive to the longevity of the flower’s attributes.
