About Cannabis Edibles

When THC is ingested through edibles instead of inhalation, the resulting effects are notably different. Explore the unique dynamics of cannabis edibles and the heightened experience they offer compared to other consumption methods.

Discover a variety of cannabis edibles at Highway 90 Dispensary, including gummies, chocolates, baked goods, and more. Edibles offer a discreet and flavorful way to enjoy cannabis, with options available in different strengths and formulations. Our menu includes edibles suited for both new and experienced consumers, making it easy to find something that fits your comfort level.

History of Cannabis Edibles

Cannabis edibles boast a global history spanning centuries, reaching back to the 10th century in India with the introduction of a drink called Bhang. This initial edible featured pulverized cannabis flower, ghee, milk, and spices, serving as both a sleep aid and appetite enhancer. In the United States, a pivotal moment in cannabis edibles’ history occurred in the 1960s with the publication of Alice B. Tokias’s book, which included a recipe for “Hashish Fudge.” This marked the early emergence of weed edibles in American culture, leading to the widespread baking of pot brownies in kitchens across the nation.

How are Cannabis Edibles Made?

Most edibles are crafted with cannabis-infused butter or oil, combining ground flower with a chosen cooking medium and applying heat to activate cannabinoids through decarboxylation. Today, edibles stand as one of the most favored cannabis consumption methods, especially among seasoned users with higher tolerances and those seeking the health benefits of cannabis. Virtually every imaginable food product has a THC-infused counterpart. Tinctures add versatility, allowing you to transform any food or beverage into an edible with a few droplets of liquid THC.

Benefits of Cannabis Edibles

One key factor contributing to the popularity of edibles is their sustained impact on the system, delivering the “high” experience for four to eight hours post-consumption. This extended duration proves particularly advantageous for cannabis users grappling with physical pain symptoms arising from injuries or various ailments. The choice to consume cannabis through edibles is especially valuable for individuals with sensitive respiratory systems or those who prefer to avoid smoking.

Cannabis Edibles FAQs

What do Cannabis Edibles look like?

Cannabis-infused edibles come in a plethora of delicious forms, with THC seamlessly incorporated into nearly every favorite snack imaginable. While the options are extensive, the most popular shapes include gummies, chocolates, hard candies, and classic baked goods like brownies and cookies. Beyond these classics, emerging edibles encompass syrups, beverages such as sodas or teas, and water-soluble packets or tinctures that can be effortlessly added to any drink.

How long does it take for Cannabis Edibles to kick in?

Edible effects can take anywhere from 30 minutes to 2 hours, depending on the individual. We also carry fast-acting edibles that offer customers a quicker onset of effects relative to traditional edibles.

How long do Cannabis Edibles high last?

Typically, the impact of cannabis edibles can linger for 4 to 8 hours, with variables like dosage, tolerance, metabolism, and the specific product influencing the duration. To ensure a seamless experience, begin with a low dose, exercise patience, and allow sufficient time for the effects to manifest.

Are Cannabis Edibles safer than smoked cannabis?

Cannabis edibles present distinct advantages compared to smoked cannabis. By sidestepping the risks associated with smoking, such as lung irritation and exposure to toxins, edibles offer a safer consumption method. Additionally, they deliver a prolonged and gradual effect, ensuring a controlled and predictable experience. Keep in mind, though, that edibles may have a delayed onset and potent effects.

How many Cannabis Edibles should I take to feel it?

For an optimal experience, commence with a low dose and adjust gradually based on factors like tolerance, metabolism, and individual sensitivity. Check the product’s packaging for serving-size recommendations. As edibles have a slower onset compared to other cannabis forms, seek guidance from a knowledgeable budtender if uncertain.

What Cannabis Edibles dose is right for me?

The most important part of edible consumption is taking an appropriate dosage based on your tolerance and the experience you intend to have. Here’s a quick guide to dosing:

  • 1 – 2.5mg: For new users and microdosers.
  • 5mg: For recreational users, this is a great dose for a gentle high and slight euphoria, great for aiding symptoms of restless sleepers, and for those battling eating disorders.
  • 10 – 15mg: For users with a higher THC tolerance, this dose offers stronger euphoria and symptom relief, and users can sometimes experience drowsiness.
  • 20 – 30mg: For experienced consumers with significant THC tolerance, at this dosage users who are not as familiar with edibles can possibly experience negative side effects.
  • 50mg and above: For very experienced users with significantly high tolerance, or patients using these edibles as pain management for cancer treatments, inflammatory disorders, and other medical ailments.
